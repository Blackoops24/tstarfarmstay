House rules
  • No illegal activity (narcotics etc.) or events allowed.
  • Check in strictly after 1 PM and check out by 10:30 AM.
  • Please carry your own towels.
  • No loud music after 8 PM.
  • Breakages are charged (glasses, TV, mirrors, chairs etc.).
  • Do not use slippers/shoes inside the house.
  • Switch off lights/fans, indoors and outdoors, when not in use.
  • Smoking is strictly not allowed inside the house; outdoors is fine.
  • Avoid plastic waste and keep the property clean.
Home rules & truths
  • Non-vegetarian food is allowed.
  • Please treat the home with care.
  • Children must be supervised in and around the pool.
  • Wear swimming attire in the pool and shower before entering.
  • Alcohol is allowed in designated areas only. Please do not carry food/drinks in or around the pool.
  • The villa is in a secluded location and is a standalone property.
  • The caretaker stays on the premises.
  • CCTV cameras cover the external areas for security.
Other details to note
  • This is not a resort – please don't expect five-star amenities.
  • Plunge / party pool size: L 12 ft × W 4.5 ft.
  • During monsoon there may be insects and bugs around the property.
  • Power outage: inverter backup for up to 5 hours.
  • Pet friendly; not wheelchair friendly.
  • A cot can be provided for up to 8 guests; extra floor mattresses are available for additional guests.
  • We advise accompanying the caretaker when venturing out, for safety and guidance.
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi, subject to network availability.
